Privacy policy

Updated 8 October 2026

Yomikaki doesn’t collect any data about you. Everything it keeps stays on your iPhone.

What Yomikaki keeps

Your answers and how long they took, your progress on each kana, and a few settings (the script and mode you last chose, and whether you’ve seen the introduction). They’re stored on your iPhone, inside the app, so it can schedule your reviews and show your stats.

What it doesn’t do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No network connections: nothing you do in the app is sent to me or to anyone else.

Apple

Like any app’s data, Yomikaki’s is included in your iPhone’s backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) if you have them turned on. If you’ve chosen in Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Both are handled by Apple under its privacy policy.

Links

The About screen links to KanjiVG and to the licences of the stroke data and fonts. They open in your browser, and those sites have their own policies.

Deleting your data

In Stats, Reset progress deletes your progress and answer history, for one script and mode or for everything. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

Yomikaki collects no data from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version works differently (syncing your progress through your own iCloud account, for example), this page will say so before that version is released, and the date above will change.
