Kana, one stroke at a time.

Yomikaki (読み書き, “reading and writing”) is a free iPhone app for learning to read and write hiragana and katakana, a few kana at a time.

  • Reading: see a kana and type its romaji. It’s checked as you type.
  • Writing: draw it on the practice grid. Trace it, copy it, then write it from memory, with the stroke order every time.
  • Each kana comes back when it’s due, so a little practice each day goes a long way.

Questions

Where is my progress kept?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. Yomikaki has no account and sends nothing over the internet. See the privacy policy.

I already know some kana.

Until you’ve practiced a script, home offers a placement check: two kana from each row, and every row you pass opens straight away. A new kana you get right the first time counts as known too.

My drawing was graded wrong.

Writing is graded for you, but you always have the last word: after each attempt you can change the grade.

How do I start over?

In Stats, choose Reset progress. You can reset the script and mode on screen, or everything.
